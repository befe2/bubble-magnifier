# Privacy Policy for Bubble Magnifier

**Effective Date: September 27, 2026**

Bubble Magnifier is an Android screen magnification application developed by Befekadu Mengistu. This Privacy Policy explains how Bubble Magnifier handles information when you use the application.

## Screen Content and Magnification

Bubble Magnifier uses Android screen-capture functionality, including MediaProjection, to provide real-time screen magnification.

Screen content used for magnification is processed locally on the user's device. Bubble Magnifier does not transmit captured screen content to the developer or to external servers.

## Accessibility Service

Bubble Magnifier uses Android AccessibilityService functionality to provide its system-wide magnification and interaction features.

The AccessibilityService is used only to support magnification functionality and related user interactions. Bubble Magnifier does not use accessibility functionality for advertising, profiling, or the sale of user information.

## Information Collection and Sharing

Bubble Magnifier does not require users to create an account or sign in.

The current version of Bubble Magnifier does not contain advertising or analytics services and does not transmit personal information to the developer.

Bubble Magnifier does not sell personal information or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

## Local App Settings

Certain preferences and settings, including custom magnifier shape information and application state, may be stored locally on the user's device so that selected settings can be restored.

These locally stored settings are not transmitted to the developer.

## Permissions and System Access

Bubble Magnifier may request Android permissions or system authorization necessary to provide screen magnification and related functionality.

Users control applicable permissions through Android and can revoke them through their device settings.

## Data Retention

Screen content processed for magnification is not stored by Bubble Magnifier as a remote user-data record.

Locally stored application preferences remain on the device according to Android's application-storage behavior and can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

## Children's Privacy

Bubble Magnifier is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Bubble Magnifier is designed to perform its magnification processing locally on the user's device, minimizing transmission of user information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Bubble Magnifier's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
